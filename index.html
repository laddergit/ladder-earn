Vote Roadmap — Solana

Vote-escrowed LADDER (veLADDER), the Fables-style model Earn was designed around — rebuilt for Solana, in the open, in the right order.

Vote is not live yet — and this page will not pretend otherwise

On EVM chains, vote-escrow comes from standard ve contracts. Solana has no vote-escrow primitive: veLADDER needs a custom Anchor program — written, tested and deployed from Hasan's wallet first. The sequence is fixed: Solana $LADDER token → Ladder Earn's own Raydium pools → ve program → Vote live. Until then nothing on this page can be locked, voted or claimed, every figure is a dash and every voting control is disabled.

1 · Lock

Lock $LADDER for up to 2 years to mint veLADDER. Longer locks mean more voting power, decaying linearly to expiry — the same model as the EVM design, re-implemented as an Anchor program.

2 · Vote

Each week, direct veLADDER at the pools you want emissions to flow to. Pool weights settle at the epoch boundary.

3 · Earn

Pools that win votes receive LADDER emissions, paid to their LPs. Bribes and fee-sharing arrive with the program, not before.

4 · Status

Waiting on step one: the Solana $LADDER token and Ladder Earn's own Raydium pools. This tab turns live only when the ve program is deployed and its program ID is published here.

How routing works here

Earn does not hold its own routing contracts on Solana — Jupiter is the aggregator. Earn sends your pair and amount to Jupiter, Jupiter compares Raydium, Orca, PumpSwap and every other integrated venue in one call, and the winning route is what the Swap tab executes. Routing never moves another DEX's liquidity; it sends the trade to wherever the pool already is.

Pick a pair and an amount in the Swap tab — Earn asks Jupiter once, with your slippage tolerance attached.

Jupiter's route plan names every venue and hop it considered; the Swap panel shows the winning route before you sign.

The built transaction is simulated against Solana mainnet. If simulation is not green, Earn refuses with the reason instead of sending.

You sign in Phantom or Solflare; Earn sends the signed transaction and confirms it on-chain. Funds move wallet-to-pool, never through Earn.
