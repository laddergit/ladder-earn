How veLDR will work

1 · Lock $LADDER → veLDR

Lock $LADDER for a chosen period to receive veLDR. The longer the lock, the more voting power each $LADDER carries. veLDR is non-transferable and decays toward the unlock date.

2 · Weekly epochs

Voting runs in weekly epochs. During an epoch, veLDR holders allocate their voting power across the pools they want to support.

3 · Votes direct emissions

Future $LADDER emissions are planned to flow to pools in proportion to the votes they receive, so liquidity follows what voters actually back.

4 · Voters earn

Pools can attach incentives for voters, and veLDR holders are planned to share in the fees of the pools they vote for.

Planned veLDR parameters

The veLDR design Ladder will adopt, stated in full so the model is fixed before any contract ships. Nothing below is live yet.

Locking

Lock $LADDER for 1–104 weeks, or choose Auto-max to keep the lock at full length automatically. veLDR is non-transferable and its power decays toward the unlock date.

Voting weight

Weight = locked $LADDER × weeks remaining ÷ 105. A fresh 104-week lock starts at nearly full weight; shorter or older locks carry proportionally less.

Epochs

Voting runs in weekly epochs, Tuesday 00:00 UTC to the next Tuesday. A lock's vote can split across up to 10 markets, persists week to week until changed, and is time-weighted within the week.

Changing your vote

The first vote each week is free. Re-votes cost 2.63% of that week's voter income, rising evenly to 50% at the twentieth re-vote.

What voters earn

Voters earn the swap fees of staked liquidity in the markets they vote for, plus the claim fee, plus any posted incentives — paid in the token earned. Rewards expire 26 weeks after their week's payout.

Claim fee

Unstaked positions pay a 10% claim fee on their swap fees, which goes to voters. The contract caps this fee at 20%.

Emissions budgets

Votes set each market's emissions budget, capped per market at 5 × (eligible staked fees + incentives), so emissions cannot outrun what a market actually earns.

Rebase

A rebase adds 8% of LP emissions to voting locks while 30% or less of supply is locked, sliding to 0% once 90% or more of supply is locked.

The routing layer is live on mainnet — verified routers quote side by side

Routing never moves another DEX's liquidity; it sends the trade to wherever the pool already is. Every venue sits behind one adapter interface and the quotes below are live on-chain reads. Earn is aggregator-first on mainnet: Ladder's own contracts are not deployed yet, so four verified external venues answer — DEX 0x7272…1422 (the main venue, 22 WHYPE pairs including the graduated launchpad tokens), DEX 0xc48c…8715, DEX 0xbbbc…5df2 and DEX 0x8cec…48df — each verified on-chain (factory() and WETH() bindings exact against the canonical mainnet WHYPE, quote math matched to the wei, swap simulation passed), each executing through its own router. Seven more factories are tracked view-only: the deepest pool on the chain (DONTBUY/WHYPE) sits on a venue whose only swap contract is nonstandard, and the rest exposed no verifiable standard router. Honest depth warning: total V2 liquidity on mainnet is still thin (~100 HYPE across all factories at the 9 Nov census) — the Uniswap V4 adapter below now covers the PoolManager pools where most of the chain's liquidity sits, including the Montra launchpad pools — expect price-impact warnings on larger trades.

How the framework works

1 · Registry

Every venue is one config entry: name, protocol kind, router and factory addresses, and which adapter speaks to it. Adding a DEX — or Ladder itself, once deployed — means adding an entry; nothing else in the app changes.

2 · Adapters

Each protocol family gets one adapter behind a single interface — quote(amountIn, path) and buildSwap(quote). The V2 adapter is live below, and the V4 adapter (Quoter for pricing, Universal Router for execution) sits in the same shape beside it.

3 · Compare

All live adapters answer the same request in one batch — direct pairs and multi-hop paths through HYPE. The winner is the highest net output after that venue's own swap fees, not the best headline price.

4 · Execute & fall back

The winning quote becomes a swap through that venue's own router in the Swap tab, guarded by the user's slippage tolerance. If a venue's router stalls or reverts, the comparison falls back to the next-best quote.
