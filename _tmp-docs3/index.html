How the products fit together

Earn is Ladder's main app and where liquidity lives: it aggregates Solana's pools and routes swaps through Jupiter. Swap and Pools are its two core actions — the landing page links to them, and Earn is the app behind both.

One honest line about status: Earn is live on Solana mainnet with live Raydium markets and live Jupiter quotes; a wallet-signed swap or deposit from a real Phantom wallet has not been proven end-to-end yet. Each section below repeats the status that applies to it. The $LADDER token itself has not launched on Solana yet either; its route will be announced by @ladderswap when it's decided.

Getting Started

You need a Solana wallet and a little SOL. SOL pays the network fee on every transaction — keep some spare, because a transaction with no SOL for fees simply can't be sent.

  1. Install a Solana wallet

    Phantom or Solflare — both inject into the page, so Earn can find them without any extra setup. Back up your recovery phrase offline before you fund the wallet; never paste it into any website, including this one.

  2. Fund it with SOL

    Buy SOL on an exchange and withdraw it to your wallet address, or receive it from another wallet. SOL on mainnet is a real asset — there is no faucet. A small amount covers many transactions; keep more if you plan to provide liquidity.

  3. Connect

    Open earn.ladderswap.trade and click Connect. Your wallet asks you to approve the connection — approving a connection does not move any funds.

  4. Simulate before every send

    Before any transaction is sent, it is simulated first (simulateTransaction). If the simulation fails or shows an unexpected balance change, the transaction is not sent — you'll see the error instead of signing blind. Read every wallet prompt before you approve it.

  5. You're ready

    Browse Markets, make a swap, or add your first liquidity position.

Markets

The Markets page is a single table of liquidity pools on Solana mainnet. The pool list comes from Raydium CPMM — constant-product pools, the closest Solana equivalent of a classic two-token pool: no price ranges to manage, deposits are ratio-locked, and you hold an LP token for your share. Prices and token metadata come from Jupiter. The list is live: pools, reserves and volumes are read from Raydium's live data at the moment you look.

Not every pool Raydium knows about is listed. A pool only appears if it passes Earn's sanity filters — it must have real trading volume (day volume above zero) and a minimum TVL. The raw top-by-TVL list is polluted by zero-volume wash tokens, so Earn filters by activity rather than showing you a leaderboard of dead pools.

Why a dash instead of 0%?

APR needs a time window to mean anything. Earn snapshots fee growth and refuses to annualise a few minutes of activity into a fantasy number. If a pool is new, its Earn-measured APR simply appears once an hour of data exists.

Swap

Earn's swap is powered by Jupiter, Solana's swap aggregator. Instead of quoting a single pool, Jupiter searches across Solana's venues and returns the best route for your pair and amount — Earn then walks you through signing and sending it.

How a swap works

  1. Quote

    Pick a pair and an amount. Earn requests a quote from Jupiter's Swap API (lite-api.jup.ag) — the quote returns the expected output, the route plan across venues, the price impact and the minimum received at your slippage setting.

  2. Build the transaction

    Jupiter builds a ready-to-sign transaction for that exact quote — a versioned transaction with SOL wrapping and unwrapping handled for you when one side is native SOL.

  3. Simulate, then sign

    The transaction is simulated before your wallet is ever prompted. If the simulation fails, nothing is sent. If it passes, your wallet (Phantom / Solflare) shows the transaction for your approval — check the amounts, then sign.

  4. Send & confirm

    The signed transaction is sent to the network and confirmed. The result links to the transaction on Solscan, where anyone can verify exactly what moved.

Slippage & price impact

  • Slippage tolerance — your swap fails rather than fills if the price moves against you by more than your setting before it lands. The "minimum received" figure in the review is your amount out after slippage.
  • Price impact — how much your own trade moves the pool price. Jupiter reports it with every quote; on thin pools, consider splitting the trade or reducing the size.
  • No token approvals to manage. Solana doesn't use ERC-20-style allowances — your signature on the transaction is the authorisation, and it covers exactly that transaction. Anything asking you to sign a separate "unlimited approval" is not Earn.
Honest status

Quotes are live — Jupiter's quote endpoint answers from mainnet in real time. But a wallet-signed swap sent from a real Phantom wallet has not been proven end-to-end yet, so treat the first swaps as early: start small, and check the simulation result and the Solscan record every time.

Pools & Liquidity

Providing liquidity means depositing a pair of tokens into a pool so others can swap against it. In return you receive LP tokens representing your share of the pool. Earn's pools are Raydium CPMM pools — constant-product, no price ranges, deposits locked to the pool's current ratio.

Add liquidity

  1. Choose a pool

    Pick a pair from Markets. Only pools that pass Earn's volume and TVL filters are listed.

  2. Enter amounts

    Deposits follow the pool's current price ratio: enter one side and Earn fills the matching amount of the other token. For a brand-new pool, your deposit sets the starting price — double-check it against a reference price.

  3. Simulate, sign & supply

    The deposit transaction is simulated first; if it passes, your wallet prompts you to sign. You receive LP tokens proportional to your share of the pool.

Remove liquidity

Choose how much of your position to withdraw (up to 100%) and confirm. Your LP tokens are burned and you receive both tokens back in the pool's current ratio, including your share of the fees the pool has collected.

How the swap fee works — honestly

Every swap through a CPMM pool pays the pool's configured fee (standard CPMM fee tiers are 0.25%, 1% and 4%). There is no separate fee vault and no claim button: the fee never leaves the pool. It stays in the reserves, so each LP token becomes redeemable for slightly more of both tokens than before. Your earnings are realised when you withdraw — the longer fees accumulate, the more your LP tokens are worth relative to what you deposited.

Impermanent loss

If the price of one token in the pair moves a lot relative to the other, you can withdraw less value than if you had just held the two tokens. Fees compensate for this, but they don't eliminate it. Only provide liquidity in pairs you understand.

Honest status

The pool list, reserves and volumes are live mainnet data. A wallet-signed deposit or withdrawal from a real Phantom wallet has not been proven end-to-end yet — the same caution as swaps applies: start small and verify on Solscan.

Portfolio

The Portfolio page is your position overview for the connected wallet:

  • LP positions — every tracked CPMM pool where your wallet holds LP tokens, your share of the pool, and what that share is currently redeemable for. Positions are discovered from your wallet's token accounts, not from an Earn account — there is no Earn account.
  • Balances — your SOL and SPL token balances, read from the chain for the connected wallet.
  • Fees earned (estimate) — the difference between what your position is worth now and the value you deposited, attributed to accumulated swap fees. It's an estimate: price movement between deposit and now also affects it, and the figure is computed from live reserves, not promised anywhere on-chain as a separate balance.
Nothing is custodied

LP tokens live in your own wallet, not in Earn. Disconnecting, switching devices or never opening Earn again doesn't change your positions — withdraw any time by reconnecting the same wallet.

Vote / veLADDER Phase 2 — roadmap, not live

Vote is the planned governance and emissions-direction layer for Ladder, following the ve(3,3) model. It is a phase-2 roadmap item: there is no locking and no voting today. On Solana there is no built-in vote-escrow primitive, so Vote needs a custom on-chain program — written, deployed, and wired into the app — before any of this can exist. The sequence is fixed: $LADDER token first, then Ladder's own pools, then the ve program, then Vote goes live.

The planned model

  • Lock $LADDER, get veLADDER. Lock $LADDER for between 1 and 104 weeks, or choose auto-max to keep the lock permanently at maximum length.
  • Voting weight decays with time unless you extend or auto-max:
weight = locked $LADDER × weeks remaining ÷ 105
  • Vote on pool incentives. Each epoch, veLADDER holders direct emissions toward the pools they vote for; pools that attract votes receive a larger share of that epoch's incentives.
  • Epochs run weekly. Votes and weights are snapshot per epoch.
Roadmap, not a promise of figures

The parameters above are the design Earn is being built toward. Emission rates, fee sharing and launch timing will be announced once the $LADDER token exists and the ve program is deployed — nothing on this page is a live yield or a return guarantee.

Aggregator

Earn is an aggregator over Solana's liquidity, in two layers. Swaps route through Jupiter, which searches Solana's venues for the best route on every quote. Markets and liquidity read Raydium pools directly — CPMM in v1 — with pool data from Raydium's API and prices and token metadata from Jupiter. Nothing is routed through a Ladder contract: swaps execute through Jupiter's chosen venue, and deposits go straight into the Raydium pool.

LayerStatusNotes
Jupiter — swap routing Live Quote → swap transaction → sign → send, via lite-api.jup.ag. Quotes are live; wallet-signed sends not yet proven end-to-end.
Raydium CPMM — pools & LP Live Live pool list with TVL, volume and fees. Deposit and withdraw are LP-token based, ratio-locked, simulated before signing.
Raydium CLMM / Orca Whirlpools Later phase Concentrated liquidity with NFT positions. Deferred — v1 covers CPMM only, deliberately.

Two rules hold across every layer: figures come from on-chain reserves and events (see Analytics), and every transaction is simulated before it is sent. API keys are never embedded in the page — Earn uses the public, keyless endpoints only.

Analytics

Every number in Earn is computed from chain data or labelled with where it came from. There is no admin-entered figure and no smoothing to make a pool look busier than it is.

The rules

  • Reserves are read live. TVL and pool prices come from the pools' current on-chain reserves; token prices and metadata come from Jupiter.
  • Volume and fees come from pool activity. They are derived from the venue's reported trading over the window shown — day, week or month — never extrapolated from a few minutes.
  • APR is Earn-measured only with history. Fee-growth snapshots are taken over time; a pool's Earn APR appears only once at least 1 hour of snapshots exists. Until then, an APR figure is the venue-reported one and is labelled as such — Earn does not pass Raydium's number off as its own measurement.
  • Pools are filtered, not padded. A pool needs day volume above zero and a minimum TVL to be listed at all, so the table doesn't fill up with wash tokens that happen to show a large TVL.
  • Estimates are labelled as estimates. Portfolio fee earnings are derived by comparing your position's current redeemable value with your deposits — price movement is part of that number, and the docs and the UI both say so.
Verify it yourself

Every pool, position and transaction can be checked independently on Solscan using the program IDs and mint addresses in the reference section. If a figure in Earn ever disagrees with the chain, the chain is right.

Risks

Read before you swap or deposit anything

Mainnet means real assets. Everything you swap or deposit is real value on Solana mainnet. Transactions are irreversible — trade at your own risk, and only with funds you can afford to lose.

Contracts are unaudited. The programs Earn routes through (Jupiter's venues, Raydium) and Ladder's own programs, when deployed, have not been audited by Ladder. "Verified" in these docs means behaviour was checked on-chain, not that code is audited or safe.

Early software. Earn's markets and quotes are live, but wallet-signed swaps and deposits from a real wallet have not been proven end-to-end yet. Start small; verify everything on Solscan.

Thin liquidity and price impact. New and small pools can move violently on modest trades. Check the price impact on every quote, and remember a token's opening price is set by its first liquidity, not by any valuation.

Impermanent loss. Providing liquidity can return less value than simply holding the two tokens. Fees compensate; they don't eliminate it.

Verify addresses here. Only trust mint and program addresses published in Programs & Addresses and announcements from the official @ladderswap account. Never a link or address sent by a stranger.

FAQ

Are the assets on Earn real?

Yes. Earn runs on Solana mainnet: SOL, USDC and every pool token are real assets with real value, and transactions are irreversible. Markets and quotes are live; wallet-signed swaps and deposits from a real Phantom wallet have not been proven end-to-end yet, so start small.

Where is $LADDER on Solana?

It hasn't launched yet — there is no Solana mint for $LADDER today. The launch route will be announced by @ladderswap, and the mint address will be published in Programs & Addresses the moment it exists. Any $LADDER mint you see before that is not the real one.

Why does a pool show "—" for APR?

Earn-measured APR is only published after at least one hour of fee-growth snapshots exist for that pool. New or quiet pools show a dash — or a clearly labelled venue-reported figure — until there's real history to annualise. Earn doesn't extrapolate a few minutes of activity into an APR.

Where is the claim button for my LP fees?

There isn't one, by design. On CPMM pools the swap fee stays inside the pool reserves, so your LP tokens grow in redeemable value. You collect fees by withdrawing liquidity — see Pools & Liquidity.

When does Vote / veLADDER launch?

Vote is phase 2. It needs a custom on-chain program — Solana has no vote-escrow primitive — and it only makes sense after the $LADDER token exists and Ladder's own pools are live, because votes direct emissions to pools. There is no locking or voting yet and no date is promised; the parameters in the Vote section describe the planned ve(3,3) design.

Which wallet do I need?

A Solana wallet — Phantom or Solflare — with some SOL for network fees. Every transaction is simulated before it is sent; if a simulation fails, the transaction is never sent. See Getting Started.
