Ladder Earn

Ladder Earn is the liquidity app for Ladder on Elysium mainnet. Earn launched on mainnet aggregator-first: pools come from external Elysium DEXes, each badge-labelled by venue, so you can compare markets, swap, and provide liquidity in one place. Ladder's own pools join the same view once Ladder's contracts are deployed on mainnet.

LIVE ON ELYSIUM MAINNET Chain ID 99901 · Aggregator mode — real assets, trade at your own risk

Markets

One pool table for the whole chain, with a badge showing which DEX each pool belongs to. TVL, volume and APR are computed from on-chain data — see how the numbers work.

Swap

Quotes compare the verified external venues, then route directly or through a HYPE / WHYPE hop — whichever gives you more out.

Pools & Liquidity

Add or remove liquidity on verified venues' pools and hold the LP tokens in your wallet. The swap fee grows inside the pool itself.

Portfolio

Your LP positions, wallet balances and recent activity in one view, with an estimate of the fees your positions have earned.

How Earn relates to Ladder

Ladder is the exchange protocol: a factory, a router and Uniswap V2–style pools, introduced at ladderswap.trade. Ladder Earn is the primary app built on top of it. On mainnet, Earn launched aggregator-first over external Elysium DEXes, because Ladder's own mainnet Factory and Router are not deployed yet — they are listed as Pending deploy in Networks & Contracts. Once Ladder's own pools are live, they join Markets in the same view, and pools created through Ladder's Launchpad graduate into Ladder's router.

Earn previously ran on Elysium testnet; that deployment is retired and these docs now describe mainnet only.

Getting Started

You need an EVM wallet and some HYPE on Elysium mainnet for gas. HYPE on mainnet is a real asset — there is no faucet.

  1. Connect your wallet

    Open earn.ladderswap.trade and click Connect. Earn discovers installed wallets automatically (EIP-6963), so MetaMask, Rabby and other injected wallets appear in the list — pick yours and approve the connection in the wallet.

  2. Add the Elysium Mainnet network

    If your wallet doesn't know the network yet, Earn offers to add it. You can also add it manually with the details below. Approving the network add does not move any funds.

  3. Bridge HYPE to Elysium mainnet

    HYPE is the gas token. Bridge HYPE from HyperEVM to Elysium via the official bridge. Kinetiq has not published an official bridge URL yet — until it does, only use bridge links from official Kinetiq channels, and never a bridge link sent by a stranger. Mainnet has no faucet.

  4. You're ready

    Browse Markets, make a swap, or add your first liquidity position.

Markets

The Markets page is a single table of liquidity pools across Elysium mainnet. In aggregator mode the pools come from external Elysium DEXes — the verified venues' WHYPE- and USDC-anchored V2 pools, merged with view-only factories' pools — and every row carries a DEX badge (a neutral venue id such as dex-7272) so you always know whose pool you are looking at. Ladder's own pools appear in the same table once Ladder deploys on mainnet.

One honest caveat about USD figures: there is no usable WHYPE/USDC V2 pool yet — the only one that exists holds dust — so dollar values are derived through HYPE pricing rather than a deep on-chain stablecoin pair. Treat early USD figures as estimates on a young chain.

External pools

Pools on the four router-verified venues are fully tradable in Earn: swap, deposit and withdraw all execute through each venue's own router — funds never route through Ladder's router into another DEX's pair. Pools from additional factories are View-only: you can inspect their reserves, volume and fees, but you can't trade or deposit into them through Earn until their routers pass the same verification.

Why a dash instead of 0%?

APR needs a time window to mean anything. Earn snapshots fee growth and refuses to annualise a few minutes of activity into a fantasy number. If a pool is new, its APR simply appears once an hour of data exists.

Swap

Earn's swap is route-aware: instead of quoting a single pool, it asks every venue it can trade on and shows you the best result.

How quoting works

  1. Pick a pair and an amount

    Enter the token and amount you want to sell. Native HYPE is handled for you — it's wrapped to WHYPE and unwrapped behind the scenes.

  2. Venues are compared

    Earn quotes the direct pool on each verified venue, plus one-hop routes through HYPE / WHYPE. Routing hubs on HYPE/WHYPE because there is no usable WHYPE/USDC V2 pool yet (the only one is dust), so USDC pairs route via HYPE rather than directly against WHYPE. The route that returns the most of the output token wins.

  3. Review, approve, swap

    Check the expected output, price impact and minimum received, then confirm. The swap executes through the winning venue's own router. (Once Ladder's own mainnet pools are live, Ladder pools will execute through Ladder's router the same way.)

Slippage & price impact

  • Slippage tolerance — your swap reverts if the price moves against you by more than your setting before it lands. The "minimum received" figure in the review is your amount out after slippage.
  • Price impact — how much your own trade moves the pool price. Earn shows a warning when impact exceeds 1%; on thin pools, consider splitting the trade or reducing the size.
  • Approvals are exact-amount. When a token needs an allowance, Earn requests approval for the exact amount of that swap — never an unlimited approval. A new swap for a larger amount asks again.

The verified venues Verified

Four external venues are verified for trading in Earn on mainnet. Each passed the full check — its router's factory() and wrapped-native address match its factory on-chain, quotes reconcile with pool reserves, and a simulated swap succeeds. Their pools quote and execute through each venue's own router. The full registry, with copyable factory and router addresses, lives in Networks & Contracts:

  • dex-7272 — the largest verified venue, including the graduated launchpad tokens (AGORA, KLEOS, BOXA, BLOB, e/acc).
  • dex-c48c, dex-bbbc, dex-8cec — three further verified V2 venues.

Venue ids are neutral on purpose: they are derived from the factory address, not a brand claim. All other external factories remain view-only until their routers are verified the same way.

"No route" and quote timeouts

  • Quotes are given 12 seconds. If a venue can't answer in time, it's left out of that quote rather than guessed.
  • If a quote call reverts, that venue has no usable route for the pair — the result is final for that venue and won't be retried mid-quote.
  • If every venue fails, Earn shows No route. That means no pool (direct or via HYPE/WHYPE) can serve the pair right now — usually because a pool doesn't exist or has no liquidity yet.

Pools & Liquidity

Providing liquidity means depositing a pair of tokens into a pool so others can swap against it. In return you receive LP tokens representing your share of the pool. On mainnet, Earn's pools belong to the external verified venues (Ladder's own pools arrive when Ladder deploys).

Add liquidity

  1. Choose a pool on a verified venue

    Pick a pair from Markets. Deposits and withdrawals are supported on the four router-verified venues, executing through each venue's own router with ratio-locked amounts, minimums computed from the router's optimal amounts, and an eth_call simulation before your wallet is ever prompted. View-only factories' pools stay view-only.

  2. Enter amounts

    Deposits follow the pool's current price ratio: enter one side and Earn fills the matching amount of the other token. For a brand-new pool, your deposit sets the starting price — double-check it against a reference price.

  3. Approve & supply

    Approve each token for the exact deposit amount, then confirm the supply transaction. You receive LP tokens proportional to your share of the pool.

Remove liquidity

Choose how much of your position to withdraw (up to 100%), approve spending the LP tokens if asked, and confirm. You receive both tokens back in the pool's current ratio, including your share of the fees the pool has collected.

How the swap fee works — honestly

Every swap on a V2 pool pays a fee (0.3% on standard V2 pools). In a Uniswap V2 design there is no separate fee vault and no claim button: the fee never leaves the pool. It simply stays in the reserves, so each LP token becomes redeemable for slightly more of both tokens than before. Your earnings are realised when you withdraw — the longer fees accumulate, the more your LP tokens are worth relative to what you deposited.

Impermanent loss

If the price of one token in the pair moves a lot relative to the other, you can withdraw less value than if you had just held the two tokens. Fees compensate for this, but they don't eliminate it. Only provide liquidity in pairs you understand.

Portfolio

The Portfolio page is your position overview for the connected wallet:

  • LP positions — every pool across the tracked venues where your wallet holds LP tokens, your share of the pool, and what that share is currently redeemable for.
  • Fees earned (estimate) — the difference between what your position is worth now and the value you deposited, attributed to accumulated swap fees. It's an estimate: price movement between deposit and now also affects it, and the figure is computed from live reserves, not promised anywhere on-chain as a separate balance.
  • Activity — a log of your swaps, deposits and withdrawals. The activity log is kept on this device, in this browser — clear your browser data and it's gone; your on-chain history is always available in the explorer regardless.
Nothing is custodied

LP tokens live in your own wallet, not in Earn. Disconnecting, switching devices or never opening Earn again doesn't change your positions — withdraw any time by reconnecting the same wallet.

Vote / veLADDER Roadmap — not live yet

Vote is the planned governance and emissions-direction layer for Ladder, following the ve(3,3) model. It activates after the $LADDER token exists and Ladder's own pools are live — votes direct emissions to pools, so the pools have to exist first. There is no locking or voting today, and any figures shown here describe the planned parameters, not a live system.

The planned model

  • Lock $LADDER, get veLADDER. Lock $LADDER for between 1 and 104 weeks, or choose auto-max to keep the lock permanently at maximum length.
  • Voting weight decays with time unless you extend or auto-max:
weight = locked $LADDER × weeks remaining ÷ 105
  • Vote on pool incentives. Each epoch, veLADDER holders direct emissions toward the pools they vote for; pools that attract votes receive a larger share of that epoch's incentives.
  • Epochs run weekly and flip on Tuesday 00:00 UTC. Votes and weights are snapshot per epoch.
Roadmap, not a promise of figures

Parameters above are the design Earn is being built toward. Emission rates, fee sharing and launch timing will be announced once the $LADDER token and Ladder's own pools are live — nothing on this page is a live yield or a return guarantee.

Analytics

Every number in Earn is computed from chain data at the moment you look at it. There is no off-chain price feed, no admin-entered figure, and no smoothing to make a pool look busier than it is.

The rules

  • Reserves are read live. TVL and swap quotes come from the pools' current on-chain reserves, priced through HYPE / WHYPE pairs. Because the only WHYPE/USDC V2 pool is dust, USD pricing is derived via HYPE rather than read from a deep stablecoin pool — another reason early USD figures are estimates.
  • Volume and fees come from events. Earn scans Swap events over the most recent 2,000 blocks — a window imposed by the public RPC's log limit — and totals them. Older activity outside that window is not included, and the window is stated rather than hidden.
  • APR needs history. Fee-growth snapshots are taken over time; a pool's APR appears only once at least 1 hour of snapshots exists. Short-window annualisation is deliberately not done.
  • Estimates are labelled as estimates. Portfolio fee earnings are derived by comparing your position's current redeemable value with your deposits — price movement is part of that number, and the docs and the UI both say so.
Verify it yourself

Every pool, position and transaction can be checked independently in the community explorer using the contract addresses in the next section. If a figure in Earn ever disagrees with the chain, the chain is right.

FAQ & Risks

Are the assets on Earn real?

Yes. Earn runs on Elysium mainnet (chain 99901): HYPE, USDC and every pool token are real assets with real value, and trades are irreversible. Earn previously ran on Elysium testnet, where assets were valueless — that deployment is retired, and nothing from testnet carried over.

Is Ladder itself live on mainnet?

Not yet. Ladder Earn launched on mainnet aggregator-first, over four verified external DEX venues. Ladder's own Factory and Router are Pending deploy (see Networks & Contracts) — when they deploy, Ladder pools join Markets and routing like any other venue, with Ladder preferred.

Why does a pool show "—" for APR?

APR is only published after at least one hour of fee-growth snapshots exist for that pool. New or quiet pools show a dash until there's real history to annualise — Earn doesn't extrapolate a few minutes of activity into an APR.

Why did my swap show "No route"?

No venue could quote the pair: there is no direct pool and no workable hop through HYPE/WHYPE, or every route's quote reverted. It usually means the pool doesn't exist yet or has no liquidity. See Swap for how quoting decides this.

Where is the claim button for my LP fees?

There isn't one, by design. On V2 pools the swap fee stays inside the pool reserves, so your LP tokens grow in redeemable value. You collect fees by withdrawing liquidity — see Pools & Liquidity.

When does Vote / veLADDER launch?

Vote activates after the $LADDER token exists and Ladder's own pools are live — votes direct emissions to pools, so both pieces have to be in place first. There is no locking or voting yet, and no date is promised in these docs — announcements will come from @ladderswap when it's ready. The parameters in the Vote section describe the planned ve(3,3) design.

Risks

Read before you deposit anything, anywhere

Mainnet means real assets. Everything you swap or deposit on Earn is real value on Elysium mainnet. Trades are irreversible — trade at your own risk, and only with funds you can afford to lose.

Unaudited contracts. The venues Earn aggregates — and Ladder's own contracts, when deployed — have not been audited by Ladder. Verification in Earn means a router's behaviour was checked on-chain, not that its code is audited or safe.

Young chain, thin liquidity. Elysium mainnet liquidity is shallow (total V2 depth was roughly 100 HYPE at the launch-day census), so price impact can be severe and USD figures are estimates. Check the price-impact warning before every swap.

Review every approval. Earn requests exact-amount approvals; anything asking for an unlimited approval is not Earn.

Verify addresses here. Only trust contract addresses published in the Networks & Contracts section of these docs and the official @ladderswap account.
