Ladder Earn

Ladder Earn is the liquidity app for Ladder, the Uniswap V2–style exchange on Elysium. It puts every pool on Elysium in one place — Ladder's own pools alongside pools from other DEXes — so you can compare markets, swap, and provide liquidity without hopping between apps.

Markets

One pool table for the whole chain, with a badge showing which DEX each pool belongs to. TVL, volume and APR are computed from on-chain data — see how the numbers work.

Swap

Quotes compare Ladder pools and verified external venues, then route directly or through a HYPE / tUSD hop — whichever gives you more out.

Pools & Liquidity

Add or remove liquidity on Ladder pools and hold the LP tokens in your wallet. The 0.3% swap fee grows inside the pool itself.

Portfolio

Your LP positions, wallet balances and recent activity in one view, with an estimate of the fees your positions have earned.

How Earn relates to Ladder

Ladder is the exchange protocol: a factory, a router and Uniswap V2–style pools, introduced at ladderswap.trade. Ladder Earn is the primary app built on top of it. Pools created through Ladder's Launchpad graduate into Ladder's router, so they show up in Earn's Markets automatically. Earn adds the multi-DEX market view, route-aware quoting and portfolio tracking around those pools.

Everything in these docs is written network-agnostic: the guides stay the same on mainnet. Only the Networks & Contracts section and the status labels change when networks change.

Getting Started

You need an EVM wallet and some testnet HYPE for gas. The whole setup takes a couple of minutes.

  1. Connect your wallet

    Open earn.ladderswap.trade and click Connect. Earn discovers installed wallets automatically (EIP-6963), so MetaMask, Rabby and other injected wallets appear in the list — pick yours and approve the connection in the wallet.

  2. Add the Elysium Testnet network

    If your wallet doesn't know the network yet, Earn offers to add it. You can also add it manually with the details below. Approving the network add does not move any funds.

  3. Get testnet HYPE

    HYPE is the gas token. Claim free testnet HYPE from the Elysium faucet linked in the app — testnet HYPE is valueless and exists only to pay gas and to trade against test tokens like tUSD.

  4. You're ready

    Browse Markets, make a swap, or add your first liquidity position.

External pools

Most external pools are View-only in Earn: you can inspect their reserves, volume and fees, but you can't deposit into or withdraw from another DEX's pool through Earn, and your funds never route through Ladder's router into another DEX's pair. The one exception is the verified external router covered in Swap, whose pools can be quoted and traded through that DEX's own router.

Why a dash instead of 0%?

APR needs a time window to mean anything. Earn snapshots fee growth and refuses to annualise a few minutes of activity into a fantasy number. If a pool is new, its APR simply appears once an hour of data exists.

Swap

Earn's swap is route-aware: instead of quoting a single pool, it asks every venue it can trade on and shows you the best result.

How quoting works

  1. Pick a pair and an amount

    Enter the token and amount you want to sell. Native HYPE is handled for you — it's wrapped to WHYPE and unwrapped behind the scenes.

  2. Venues are compared

    Earn quotes the direct pool on Ladder, the direct pool on the verified external venue, and one-hop routes through HYPE or tUSD on each. The route that returns the most of the output token wins.

  3. Review, approve, swap

    Check the expected output, price impact and minimum received, then confirm. The swap executes through the winning venue's own router — Ladder's router for Ladder pools, the external DEX's router for its pools.

Slippage & price impact

  • Slippage tolerance — your swap reverts if the price moves against you by more than your setting before it lands. The "minimum received" figure in the review is your amount out after slippage.
  • Price impact — how much your own trade moves the pool price. Earn shows a warning when impact exceeds 1%; on thin pools, consider splitting the trade or reducing the size.
  • Approvals are exact-amount. When a token needs an allowance, Earn requests approval for the exact amount of that swap — never an unlimited approval. A new swap for a larger amount asks again.

The verified external router Verified

Exactly one external venue is verified for trading in Earn today. Its pools — including the NVDAx and TSLAx markets — quote and execute through that DEX's own router:

All other external factories remain view-only until their routers are verified the same way.

"No route" and quote timeouts

  • Quotes are given 12 seconds. If a venue can't answer in time, it's left out of that quote rather than guessed.
  • If a quote call reverts, that venue has no usable route for the pair — the result is final for that venue and won't be retried mid-quote.
  • If every venue fails, Earn shows No route. That means no pool (direct or via HYPE/tUSD) can serve the pair right now — usually because a pool doesn't exist or has no liquidity yet.

Pools & Liquidity

Providing liquidity means depositing a pair of tokens into a Ladder pool so others can swap against it. In return you receive LP tokens representing your share of the pool.

Add liquidity

  1. Choose a Ladder pool

    Pick a pair from Markets, or create the pool if it's the pair's first one. Deposits and withdrawals are supported on Ladder pools only — external pools stay view-only.

  2. Enter amounts

    Deposits follow the pool's current price ratio: enter one side and Earn fills the matching amount of the other token. For a brand-new pool, your deposit sets the starting price — double-check it against a reference price.

  3. Approve & supply

    Approve each token for the exact deposit amount, then confirm the supply transaction. You receive LP tokens proportional to your share of the pool.

Remove liquidity

Choose how much of your position to withdraw (up to 100%), approve spending the LP tokens if asked, and confirm. You receive both tokens back in the pool's current ratio, including your share of the fees the pool has collected.

How the 0.3% fee works — honestly

Every swap on a Ladder pool pays a 0.3% fee. In a Uniswap V2 design there is no separate fee vault and no claim button: the fee never leaves the pool. It simply stays in the reserves, so each LP token becomes redeemable for slightly more of both tokens than before. Your earnings are realised when you withdraw — the longer fees accumulate, the more your LP tokens are worth relative to what you deposited.

Impermanent loss

If the price of one token in the pair moves a lot relative to the other, you can withdraw less value than if you had just held the two tokens. Fees compensate for this, but they don't eliminate it. Only provide liquidity in pairs you understand.

Portfolio

The Portfolio page is your position overview for the connected wallet:

  • LP positions — every Ladder pool where your wallet holds LP tokens, your share of the pool, and what that share is currently redeemable for.
  • Fees earned (estimate) — the difference between what your position is worth now and the value you deposited, attributed to accumulated swap fees. It's an estimate: price movement between deposit and now also affects it, and the figure is computed from live reserves, not promised anywhere on-chain as a separate balance.
  • Activity — a log of your swaps, deposits and withdrawals. The activity log is kept on this device, in this browser — clear your browser data and it's gone; your on-chain history is always available in the explorer regardless.
Nothing is custodied

LP tokens live in your own wallet, not in Earn. Disconnecting, switching devices or never opening Earn again doesn't change your positions — withdraw any time by reconnecting the same wallet.

Vote / veLDR Planned for mainnet — not live yet

Vote is the planned governance and fee-direction layer for Ladder, following the ve(3,3) model. It is part of the mainnet roadmap: there is no locking, voting or veLDR on testnet today, and any figures shown here describe the planned parameters, not a live system.

The planned model

  • Lock LDR, get veLDR. Lock LDR for between 1 and 104 weeks, or choose auto-max to keep the lock permanently at maximum length.
  • Voting weight decays with time unless you extend or auto-max:
weight = locked LDR × weeks remaining ÷ 105
  • Vote on pool incentives. Each epoch, veLDR holders direct emissions toward the pools they vote for; pools that attract votes receive a larger share of that epoch's incentives.
  • Epochs run weekly and flip on Tuesday 00:00 UTC. Votes and weights are snapshot per epoch.
Roadmap, not a promise of figures

Parameters above are the design Earn is being built toward for mainnet. Emission rates, fee sharing and launch timing will be announced with the mainnet release — nothing on this page is a live yield or a return guarantee.

Analytics

Every number in Earn is computed from chain data at the moment you look at it. There is no off-chain price feed, no admin-entered figure, and no smoothing to make a pool look busier than it is.

The rules

  • Reserves are read live. TVL and swap quotes come from the pools' current on-chain reserves, priced against HYPE / tUSD pairs.
  • Volume and fees come from events. Earn scans Swap events over the most recent 2,000 blocks — a window imposed by the public RPC's log limit — and totals them. Older activity outside that window is not included, and the window is stated rather than hidden.
  • APR needs history. Fee-growth snapshots are taken over time; a pool's APR appears only once at least 1 hour of snapshots exists. Short-window annualisation is deliberately not done.
  • Estimates are labelled as estimates. Portfolio fee earnings are derived by comparing your position's current redeemable value with your deposits — price movement is part of that number, and the docs and the UI both say so.
Verify it yourself

Every pool, position and transaction can be checked independently in the Elysium explorer using the contract addresses in the next section. If a figure in Earn ever disagrees with the chain, the chain is right.

FAQ & Risks

Are testnet tokens worth anything?

No. Testnet HYPE, tUSD and every pool token on chain 99801 are valueless test assets. Anyone offering to sell you testnet assets, or promising they will convert to mainnet value, is running a scam.

Will my testnet positions carry over to mainnet?

No. Mainnet is a separate chain with freshly deployed contracts. Testnet LP positions, balances and approvals stay on testnet. At mainnet launch, only the Networks & Contracts section of these docs and the status labels change — the guides stay the same.

Why does a pool show "—" for APR?

APR is only published after at least one hour of fee-growth snapshots exist for that pool. New or quiet pools show a dash until there's real history to annualise — Earn doesn't extrapolate a few minutes of activity into an APR.

Why did my swap show "No route"?

No venue could quote the pair: there is no direct pool and no workable hop through HYPE or tUSD, or every route's quote reverted. It usually means the pool doesn't exist yet or has no liquidity. See Swap for how quoting decides this.

Where is the claim button for my LP fees?

There isn't one, by design. On Ladder's V2 pools the 0.3% swap fee stays inside the pool reserves, so your LP tokens grow in redeemable value. You collect fees by withdrawing liquidity — see Pools & Liquidity.

When does Vote / veLDR launch?

Vote is planned for mainnet. There is no locking or voting on testnet, and no date is promised in these docs — announcements will come from @ladderswap when it's ready. The parameters in the Vote section describe the planned ve(3,3) design.

Risks

Read before you deposit anything, anywhere

Unaudited contracts. Ladder's contracts have not been audited. Treat testnet as an experiment, and apply the same caution to any future mainnet deployment until audits are published.

Testnet assets have no monetary value — and nothing on testnet converts to mainnet value.

Use a testnet-only wallet. Keep a separate wallet/account for testnet that never holds real funds, and review every approval your wallet asks you to sign — Earn requests exact-amount approvals, and anything asking for unlimited approval is not Earn.

Verify addresses here. Only trust contract addresses published in the Networks & Contracts section of these docs and the official @ladderswap account.
